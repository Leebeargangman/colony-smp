=====================================================================
PART 1 - YOUR FIRST TEN MINUTES
=====================================================================

You will: claim land, raise a Town Hall, meet your first two workers
and finish the three starter quests.

STEP 1 - Craft the Colony Selection Wand
  Put 1 Stick and 1 Gold Nugget side by side in any crafting grid
  (stick on the left, nugget on the right).

      [ Stick ][ Gold Nugget ]

  You get a glowing "Colony Selection Wand".

STEP 2 - Choose your land
  Walk to one corner of the land you want and LEFT-CLICK the ground
  with the wand. Green sparkles = Position 1.
  Walk to the opposite corner and RIGHT-CLICK the ground.
  White sparkles = Position 2.

  Rules:
    * At least 16 x 16 blocks, at most 128 x 128.
    * It can't overlap another colony (and needs a small gap of
      4 blocks between colonies).
    * Height doesn't matter: your claim goes from bedrock to the sky.

  While you hold the wand, your selection is outlined on the ground:
  GREEN means it can be claimed, RED means it can't. Other colonies'
  borders show in ORANGE so you can see where they are. Chat tells
  you the size and, if something is wrong, exactly what.

  Tip: a 32 x 32 to 48 x 48 claim is a comfortable start. You can't
  resize later without starting over, so think about room for farms,
  houses and a mine.

STEP 3 - Establish the colony
  Hold the wand, SNEAK (Shift) and RIGHT-CLICK. The "Establish Colony"
  menu opens and shows your land.
    * Click the green "Establish Colony" button.
    * Type your colony's name in chat (3-32 characters), or type
      "default" for "People's Commune of <you>".

  You receive two items:
    * Town Hall Core   - the heart of your colony
    * Colony Blueprint Book - for buildings (see Part 4)

  Your colony also gets a 72-HOUR PEACE SHIELD: nobody can declare
  war on you while it lasts. Use that time to build up!

STEP 4 - Place the Town Hall Core
  Place the Town Hall Core (it looks like a lodestone) anywhere inside
  your claim. Pick a central, open spot: your citizens gather here
  every evening.
    * It needs one free block beside it: the CENTRAL STATE CHEST is
      built there automatically.
    * Don't place it next to another chest.

  The Town Hall rises, the State Chest appears beside it, and your
  first two comrades walk in: a FARMER and a BUILDER.

STEP 5 - Follow the starter quests
  A yellow bar at the top of your screen shows your current quest.
  Type /colony quests to see all three.

  Quest 1 - Found the State
    Place the Town Hall Core. (Done!)

  Quest 2 - Worker Shelter
    Build an enclosed room 5 x 5 blocks counting the walls (so 3 x 3
    of floor inside), with a roof, a wooden door and 2 beds inside.
    Then hold the Blueprint Book, SNEAK and RIGHT-CLICK the floor
    inside the room. Chat says "Registered Starter House #...".
    (Prefer not to build it yourself? See "Ordering buildings" in
    Part 4: your Builder can build it for you.)

  Quest 3 - Feed the Commune
    Put 32 food items in the Central State Chest. Bread, wheat,
    carrots, potatoes, apples and meat all count.

  REWARD: hoes, an axe, a pickaxe, seeds, bone meal and bread are
  delivered straight into your State Chest.

STEP 6 - Give your Farmer a farm
  Your Farmer needs a registered farm:
    1. Make farmland with a hoe (a 9 x 9 field around one water block
       is ideal; at least 9 tiles).
    2. Hold the Blueprint Book and RIGHT-CLICK the air until chat says
       "Blueprint: Farm".
    3. SNEAK and RIGHT-CLICK the farmland.
  Make sure there are seeds in the State Chest (the quest reward has
  some). The Farmer sows, harvests and replants from now on, and every
  crop goes into the State Chest.

  Congratulations, comrade: your commune is running! Read on to grow it.


=====================================================================
PART 2 - YOUR LAND AND THE TOWN HALL
=====================================================================

YOUR CLAIM PROTECTS YOU
  Inside your claim, players who aren't members of your colony can't:
    * break or place blocks, pour or pick up liquids
    * open your chests, furnaces and other containers
    * use your doors, buttons, levers, trapdoors
    * trample your farmland, hurt your animals, armor stands or frames
    * hurt your citizens
    * fight other players (PvP is off inside claims unless the two
      colonies are at war)
  Explosions, fire spread, liquids flowing in from outside and pistons
  pushing across your border can't damage your land either.

  When you walk into a colony, the action bar tells you whose land it is.

  (The one exception is war: during a siege, attackers may break and
  place blocks in the defender's claim. See Part 10.)

THE TOWN HALL MENU
  Right-click the Town Hall Core to open the Town Hall: your control
  panel. From here you can:
    * See stability, food stock (in days), population, reputation,
      Peace Shield time, strike or mobilization status
    * Open the citizen list, the State Chest, your buildings and the
      prison
    * See your quests (and hide or show the quest bar)
    * Issue the State Mobilization Order
    * See war status and declare war
    * Set the default prisoner policy
    * See your members
    * Move overflow goods into the State Chest
    * Answer a waiting traveler

MOVING THE TOWN HALL
  The Chairman can type /colony relocate (not during a war). The core
  and the chest block are removed, you get the core back, and you can
  place it somewhere else in your claim. Nothing in the State Chest is
  lost: its contents are stored by the State, not in the block.

  Lost the core item before placing it? A leader can get a new one with
  /colony core.


=====================================================================
PART 3 - THE CENTRAL STATE CHEST
=====================================================================

  The chest beside the Town Hall is the commune's single, shared
  storehouse. Workers own nothing:
    * Everything they harvest, chop and mine goes STRAIGHT into it.
    * They take their TOOLS from it (and get a new one there when a
      tool breaks).
    * Guards take their WEAPONS, ARMOUR, BOWS and ARROWS from it.
    * Every evening the RATIONS come out of it (Part 6).
    * Builders take their BUILDING MATERIALS from it.

  Using it:
    * Right-click the chest to open it (members only). It has 3 pages
      of 45 slots; use the arrows at the bottom to switch pages.
    * /colony storage opens it from anywhere inside your claim.
    * Hoppers can feed items into it (nothing can be pulled out by
      hopper).
    * Its contents are saved by the plugin, so it works and keeps
      everything even when nobody is nearby.

  If it fills up, workers drop goods beside it and you get a warning.
  Goods that couldn't fit (quest rewards, war spoils) wait as
  "overflow": use /colony spoils (or the gold button in the Town Hall)
  to move them in once you've made room.

  Keep it stocked with:
    * FOOD (rations every evening)
    * TOOLS: hoes, axes, pickaxes (spares!)
    * SEEDS (wheat seeds, carrots, potatoes, beetroot seeds)
    * BUILDING BLOCKS: planks, cobblestone, glass, beds, doors
    * TORCHES (miners place them)
    * WEAPONS, ARMOUR, BOWS, ARROWS (for guards and emergencies)


=====================================================================
PART 4 - THE BLUEPRINT BOOK
=====================================================================

  The Colony Blueprint Book plans, registers and orders buildings.
  Citizens only use buildings that are REGISTERED with the book.
  Lost it? /colony book gives you another (or craft one: book + gold
  nugget + paper).

CONTROLS
  Hold the book ......................... see a glowing outline of the
                                          selected blueprint where you
                                          look (GREEN = fits, RED = not)
  Right-click ........................... next blueprint
  Sneak + Right-click a block ........... REGISTER a building you built
  Sneak + Left-click .................... ORDER the Builder to build
                                          the blueprint you see

  Blueprints, in order: Starter House -> Farm -> Guard Tower ->
  Prison Cell -> Mine Entrance -> (back to Starter House).

  While holding the book you also see the buildings you've already
  registered (blue outlines) and build orders (orange outlines).

THE FIVE BUILDINGS

  STARTER HOUSE - homes for your citizens
    An enclosed room (walls, floor and roof, no gaps) with at least one
    WOODEN door and at least one bed. Floor inside at least 3 x 3
    (5 x 5 counting the walls). Bigger rooms are fine.
    Register: SNEAK + RIGHT-CLICK the floor inside the room.
    Every bed is a home for one citizen. More beds = more citizens
    (children are only born when there are free beds).

  FARM - fields for your farmers
    At least 9 connected farmland (or soul sand for nether wart) tiles.
    Register: SNEAK + RIGHT-CLICK any farmland tile (or a crop on it).
    Farmers grow wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot and nether wart, and
    harvest melons and pumpkins from their stems.

  GUARD TOWER - lookout posts for your guards
    Any tower at least 5 blocks tall with room to stand on top.
    Register: SNEAK + RIGHT-CLICK the top block of the tower.
    Guards patrol between your towers. With no towers they walk a
    ring around the Town Hall.

  PRISON CELL - for prisoners (Part 8)
    An enclosed room with an IRON door and at least one bed. Floor at
    least 2 x 2 inside. Put a button outside the door so YOU can get
    in; your wardens and guards have the keys.
    Register: SNEAK + RIGHT-CLICK the floor inside the cell.
    Each bed holds one prisoner.

  MINE ENTRANCE - where miners dig
    Any natural ground: stone, dirt, grass, sand or gravel.
    Register: face the direction the mine should go, then SNEAK +
    RIGHT-CLICK the ground. Miners dig a staircase down (to around
    Y 12), then a branch mine: a main tunnel with side tunnels.
    Maximum 3 mines per colony.

  Note: a room with an iron door always registers as a Prison Cell,
  with a wooden door as a House, whichever blueprint you selected.

  Every night your buildings are re-checked. If you break a wall, take
  away the beds or dig up a farm, the building is unregistered and you
  get a message: fix it and register it again.

ORDERING BUILDINGS FROM YOUR BUILDER
  Your Builder can construct any blueprint except the Mine Entrance:
    1. Hold the book and pick the blueprint (Right-click).
    2. Look at the ground where it should go. The door faces you.
    3. Make sure the outline is GREEN (fits inside your claim, doesn't
       overlap the Town Hall or other buildings).
    4. SNEAK + LEFT-CLICK. Chat lists the materials needed.

  The Builder takes materials from the State Chest one block at a time,
  works from outside the walls, and registers the building when done.
  If something is missing, the Builder waits and tells you what to add.

  Approximate materials:
    Starter House: 69 wall/roof blocks (planks, logs or stone),
                   2 windows (glass, or more wall blocks), 1 wooden
                   door, 2 beds, 1 torch (optional)
    Prison Cell:   69 wall/roof blocks (stone preferred), 2 iron bars
                   (or glass/stone), 1 iron door, 1 bed, a torch and a
                   button (optional)
    Guard Tower:   ~38 stone blocks, 24 planks, 16 wooden fences,
                   6 ladders, 1 wooden door, 1 torch (optional)
    Farm:          tills grass/dirt for free (needs dirt where the
                   ground isn't soil) and 1 water bucket for the centre
                   (the empty bucket goes back in the chest)
  Uneven ground is filled with cobblestone or dirt from the chest.

  Up to 8 orders can wait in the queue. See or cancel them with
  /colony buildings (Shift-click an order to cancel it). With nothing
  to build, Builders cut wood.


=====================================================================
PART 5 - YOUR CITIZENS AND THEIR JOBS
=====================================================================

  Citizens are villagers with name tags like "Ivan Petrov [Farmer]".
  Right-click one to open their card:
    * Job, trait, health, how well they ate last night, work speed
      (efficiency), tool and bed
    * What they're doing right now
    * Job buttons (leaders only): click one to change their job
    * Locate: makes them glow for 15 seconds

  /colony citizens lists everyone.

THE JOBS
  FARMER ......... works registered farms. Needs seeds in the chest; a
                   hoe makes them much faster.
  BUILDER ........ builds the blueprints you order; cuts wood otherwise.
  LUMBERJACK ..... fells natural trees in and around your claim (never
                   your log houses), replants saplings and picks up the
                   saplings, sticks and apples that fall. Needs an axe.
  MINER .......... digs your registered mines for stone and ore, mines
                   out ore veins it finds, seals off lava and water,
                   places torches from the chest. NEEDS A PICKAXE (it
                   can't dig without one).
  GUARD .......... patrols towers, fights monsters, invaders and rebels,
                   escorts captives to cells, watches forced labourers.
                   Takes a weapon from the chest; in emergencies it also
                   takes the best armour, a bow and arrows.
  WARDEN ......... feeds and re-educates prisoners every day (Part 8).
                   With no prisoners, gives speeches at the Town Hall
                   that raise stability.
  UNASSIGNED ..... does nothing useful. Give them a job!

  When a citizen breaks their tool, they walk to the State Chest, take
  the best replacement and carry on. No tool in the chest? They work
  bare-handed and slowly (miners just wait), so keep spares.

TRAITS
  Every citizen has a trait, for example:
    Hardworking +20% work speed      Lazy -20% work speed
    Green Thumb +35% farming         Woodsman +35% logging
    Prospector +35% mining           Brave +30% combat damage
    Frugal eats 25% less             Glutton eats 50% more
    Stubborn / Docile: harder / easier to indoctrinate as a prisoner
  Match traits to jobs: a Green Thumb belongs on the farm!

THE DAILY ROUTINE
  Day (dawn to late afternoon) .. work
  Evening ....................... gather at the Town Hall for supper
                                  (rations are shared at sunset)
  Night ......................... sleep in their own bed
  Citizens without a bed are "homeless": they hang around the Town
  Hall at night and unhappy citizens make the commune less stable.
  Workers run away from monsters; guards stay up and keep watch.

  Downed comrades: if one of your citizens is knocked down in a fight
  (a red "DOWNED" tag), RIGHT-CLICK them within 60 seconds to help them
  up, or they bleed out.


=====================================================================
PART 6 - FOOD, STABILITY, STRIKES AND CHILDREN
=====================================================================

RATIONS AT SUNSET
  Every evening at sunset, everyone gets an EQUAL meal from the State
  Chest:
    Adult citizen ........ 6 hunger points (about one loaf of bread)
    Child ................ half
    Forced labourer ...... half
    Prisoners ............ fed by the Warden instead (Part 8)
  You get a report in chat: how well everyone ate, stability, and how
  many days of food are left.

  What counts as food (hunger points per item):
    Steak / cooked porkchop / pumpkin pie 8, bread / baked potato /
    cooked cod 5, cooked chicken / mutton / salmon 6, carrot 3,
    apple 4, wheat 1.7 (three wheat = one loaf), hay bale 15,
    raw meat 2-3, potato 1, and so on.
  Never eaten (safe to store): golden apples, golden carrots, rotten
  flesh, spider eyes, poisonous potatoes, pufferfish, chorus fruit,
  suspicious stew. Renamed items are never eaten either.
  Cheap food is eaten first, so your steak lasts.

STABILITY (0-100%)
  Stability is your commune's mood. You start at 70%.
    Full rations ............................ up (+4 a night)
    Short rations ........................... down (up to -6)
    No food at all .......................... down a lot (-10)
    Homeless citizens ....................... a little down
    Forced labourers ........................ a little down
    Idle Wardens giving speeches ............ a little up
    Mobilizing the workers .................. -5
    Winning / losing a war .................. +10 / -15
  Higher stability AND well-fed workers = faster workers. A citizen's
  villager badge shows how hard they're working.

GENERAL STRIKE
  If stability stays BELOW 35% for 3 NIGHTS IN A ROW, the workers go
  on strike. They stop working and protest at the Town Hall. The strike
  ends when stability climbs back to 50% (full rations count double
  during a strike). Chat warns you when you're getting close.

CHILDREN
  A child is born (at most one a day) when:
    * there is a FREE BED in a registered house,
    * the State Chest holds at least 3 DAYS OF FOOD for everyone,
    * stability is at least 30% and nobody is on strike.
  Children play, sleep in a bed and eat half rations. After 3 days they
  grow up and pick a useful job by themselves (you can change it).
  Population limit: 40.

  So to grow: build more houses (beds!) and keep the food stock high.

  Note: the commune only lives while someone is near the Town Hall
  (like crops, which only grow in loaded areas). Nobody has to stay
  online, and nothing is lost while you're away.


=====================================================================
PART 7 - MEMBERS AND RANKS
=====================================================================

  RANKS
    Chairman (the founder) - everything, including relocating the
                             Town Hall and dissolving the colony
    Commissar (officer)    - assigns jobs, sets prisoner policies,
                             mobilizes, declares war, invites/kicks
    Comrade (member)       - uses the State Chest, registers and orders
                             buildings, helps with everything else

  COMMANDS
    /colony invite <player>   invite someone (they click [Join])
    /colony join <colony>     accept an invitation
    /colony leave             leave your colony (not the Chairman)
    /colony kick <player>     remove a member
    /colony promote <player>  make a Comrade a Commissar
    /colony demote <player>   make a Commissar a Comrade
    /colony rename <name>     rename the colony
    /colony abandon           (Chairman) dissolve the colony; the State
                              Chest spills its contents at the chest

  You can belong to one colony at a time.


=====================================================================
PART 8 - PRISONERS: CAPTURE, INDOCTRINATION AND FORCED LABOUR
=====================================================================

  You'll need a registered PRISON CELL (Part 4) and some ROPE.

  ROPE: 3 String in a column (makes 2).
      [ String ]
      [ String ]
      [ String ]

STEP 1 - Down someone
  When a person would die in combat, there's a 20% chance they go DOWN
  instead: they lie on the ground with a red "DOWNED" tag for 60
  seconds, then bleed out. People include enemy citizens, villagers,
  wandering traders, pillagers, vindicators, evokers and witches.
  Wandering travelers always surrender instead of dying (Part 9).
  (Hitting someone who is already down finishes them.)

STEP 2 - Bind them
  RIGHT-CLICK the downed person while holding ROPE. They're bound and
  follow you on a lead. Anything they carried (weapons, armour, tools)
  goes into YOUR State Chest.

STEP 3 - Lock them up
  Either:
    * walk them into your prison cell (or right up to its door), or
    * SNEAK + RIGHT-CLICK them to hand them to your GUARDS, who walk
      them to a free cell for you.
  Chat confirms "... was locked in Prison Cell #...". If every cell is
  full, they wait (bound) until a bed is free.

STEP 4 - Choose their sentence
  Right-click the prisoner (or use /colony prison, or the Town Hall)
  and pick a policy:

  INDOCTRINATION (the default)
    Each prisoner has a RESISTANCE meter (0-100%, shown on their name
    tag). Once a day a WARDEN unlocks the cell, brings a meal from the
    State Chest and re-educates them, lowering Resistance (more when
    stability is high). At 0% they JOIN YOUR COLONY as an EQUAL CITIZEN
    with a job of their own.
      * Assign a Warden! Without one, nobody visits.
      * A day without a visit raises Resistance a little.
      * A prisoner with no food for 5 days starves. Keep food in the
        chest (you can also right-click a prisoner with food to feed
        them by hand).

  FORCED LABOUR
    Needs IRON SHACKLES (in your inventory or the State Chest):
      [ Iron Ingot ][ Chain ][ Iron Ingot ]
    The prisoner is shackled and sent to heavy labour: MINING (if you
    have a mine) or LOGGING (switch it on their card). They work during
    the day, eat half rations and are locked back in their cell at
    night.

    !!! THE GUARD RULE !!!
    Every working labourer must have a GUARD within 20 BLOCKS. Your
    guards try to keep watch automatically (one guard per work gang,
    miners and loggers being separate gangs), so have enough guards.
    You get a warning if someone is left unguarded.

    UPRISING: if a labourer is left unguarded for 30 seconds, OR your
    stability drops BELOW 40%, the labourers rise up. They break their
    shackles, steal the best weapons from your State Chest and attack
    your people. Down them and rebind them with rope, or they escape
    after 5 minutes (taking your weapons with them).

  RELEASE
    The prisoner's card has a Release button (leaders): they walk free
    and leave.

  DEFAULT POLICY
    Set what new prisoners get in the Town Hall menu or with
    /colony policy <indoctrinate|enslave>. (Forced labour as default
    still needs shackles in the chest.)


=====================================================================
PART 9 - WANDERING TRAVELERS
=====================================================================

  Now and then (during the day) a traveler with a backpack name tag
  walks to your Town Hall. Each has a trait, such as a skilled farmer.
  Chat offers clickable buttons:

    [Recruit]    They join your colony as a new citizen. You need a
                 free bed and a day of food in the State Chest for one
                 more mouth.
    [Turn Away]  They leave.

  You can also right-click them for the same choices, or type
  /colony traveler recruit | dismiss. If nobody answers in about 6
  minutes, they move on.

  TAKING THEM BY FORCE
  Attack a traveler and they surrender (go down) instead of dying. Bind
  them with rope and they become your prisoner. But word spreads:
    Attacking a traveler ............. -5 reputation
    Capturing or killing one ......... -15 reputation
    Recruiting one ................... +5 reputation
  Reputation (0-100, shown in /colony info) decides how often travelers
  come. Below 15, they stop coming altogether.


=====================================================================
PART 10 - WAR
=====================================================================

  Wars are fought between colonies over loot: the winner takes HALF of
  the loser's raw resources. Only the Chairman and Commissars can start
  (or surrender) a war.

WHO CAN BE ATTACKED
  * Colonies with a Town Hall and no PEACE SHIELD. New colonies have a
    72-hour shield; the loser of a war gets a 24-hour shield.
  * Declaring a war ENDS YOUR OWN SHIELD.
  * At least one member of the target colony must be online.
  * A colony can only be in one war at a time, and attackers must wait
    an hour between wars.

STEP 1 - Craft a WAR BANNER
  Red Banner + Iron Sword + Gold Ingot (anywhere in the grid).

STEP 2 - Declare war
  Hold the War Banner and RIGHT-CLICK THE AIR: a list of colonies
  opens (with shield status, distance and online members). Click one
  and confirm. Or type /colony declare <colony>.
  Both colonies see a red boss bar with a 20-MINUTE WARM-UP.

STEP 3 - Raise your WAR CAMP (attackers)
  During the warm-up, PLACE the War Banner OUTSIDE every claim,
  between 8 and 64 blocks from the enemy's border. This is your War
  Camp: you respawn there during the siege. If there is no camp when
  the warm-up ends, you forfeit the war.

STEP 4 - Prepare (defenders)
  * Stock the State Chest with swords, axes, armour, bows and arrows.
    When the siege starts your GUARDS run to the chest and take the
    best gear.
  * Assign more guards and build guard towers.
  * Clear the paths to your State Chest.

STEP 5 - The SIEGE (up to 45 minutes)
  Attackers may now break and place blocks in the defender's claim
  (they still can't open containers) and fight the defenders.

  ATTACKERS WIN by:
    * DESTROYING THE TOWN HALL CORE (break it), or
    * CUTTING THE SUPPLY LINES: making it impossible for the defenders'
      citizens to reach their State Chest (wall off every path, or put
      a block on its lid; SNEAK to place a block against a chest).
      Every 30 seconds without access costs the defenders 5 stability;
      at 0% the commune collapses and the attackers win.

  DEFENDERS WIN by:
    * holding out until the siege timer runs out, or
    * TEARING DOWN THE WAR CAMP (break the attackers' banner).

  Either side can type /colony surrender.

  STATE MOBILIZATION ORDER
    In an emergency (a siege, an uprising, a monster raid) a leader can
    use /colony mobilize (or the sword in the Town Hall). For 10
    minutes every farmer, builder, lumberjack and miner takes a weapon
    from the State Chest and fights as militia. Production stops and it
    costs 5 stability. /colony mobilize stop sends them back to work
    early. The order can be given again 20 minutes after the 10 minutes
    are up.

AFTER THE WAR
  * The WINNER takes 50% of the loser's RAW RESOURCES from the State
    Chest: ores, ingots, logs, crops, food and other plain stackable
    goods. Tools, armour, weapons and named items are never taken.
  * The loser gets a 24-hour Peace Shield and loses 15 stability; the
    winner gains 10.
  * Every block broken or placed during the siege is PUT BACK two
    minutes after it ends (including the Town Hall Core).

  Outside of war, other colonies' citizens can't be hurt, and if
  someone keeps attacking your citizens, your guards will fight them.


=====================================================================
PART 11 - TIPS FOR A THRIVING COMMUNE
=====================================================================

  * FOOD FIRST. Two farms early on keep everyone fed. Check "days of
    food left" in the nightly report: aim for at least 3 (that's also
    what makes children appear).
  * BEDS = GROWTH. Every house bed is a future citizen. Order Starter
    Houses from your Builder and keep planks and beds in the chest.
  * SPARE TOOLS. Keep 2-3 of each tool in the chest so nobody waits.
  * MATCH TRAITS TO JOBS. Green Thumb on the farm, Prospector in the
    mine, Brave as a guard.
  * GUARDS EARLY. Monsters wander into colonies at night. One or two
    guards with swords and a guard tower or two make a big difference.
  * DON'T STARVE YOUR STABILITY. Three bad nights in a row means a
    strike, and forced labourers rise up below 40%.
  * WARDENS ARE CHEAP STABILITY. With no prisoners they give speeches.
  * MINE FOR WEALTH. Miners bring stone, coal, iron and more. Give them
    pickaxes and torches.
  * USE YOUR SHIELD. The 72-hour Peace Shield is your time to build
    walls, towers and an armoury before anyone can attack.
  * PROTECT THE PATH TO THE CHEST. In a siege, attackers will try to
    wall it off. Don't bury your own chest in a cramped room with one
    door.


=====================================================================
PART 12 - TROUBLESHOOTING / FAQ
=====================================================================

  "My selection is red."
    Too small (under 16 x 16), too big (over 128 x 128), or too close
    to another colony. Chat tells you which.

  "I can't place the Town Hall Core."
    It must be inside your claim, with a free block beside it for the
    chest, and not next to another chest.

  "My house won't register."
    It must be fully enclosed: no missing blocks, no open doorway (use
    a door), a roof, a wooden door and a bed. Click the floor INSIDE.
    Glass panes, fences and doors count as walls.

  "My Farmer just stands around."
    Register a farm, and put seeds (wheat seeds, carrots, potatoes or
    beetroot seeds) in the State Chest.

  "My Miner says 'Needs a pickaxe'."
    Put a pickaxe in the State Chest. Also register a Mine Entrance.

  "My Lumberjack can't find trees."
    They only cut natural trees (with natural leaves) inside your claim
    or up to 24 blocks around it.

  "The Builder is waiting."
    Its card (and chat) says what it needs. Add it to the State Chest.

  "Someone is homeless."
    Register more beds in houses. Every citizen needs one.

  "Nobody visits my prisoners."
    Assign a Warden (right-click a citizen and pick Warden).

  "My workers stopped working!"
    Check for a strike (Town Hall menu or /colony info): stability has
    been under 35% for 3 nights. Feed them full rations to recover.

  "I can't declare war."
    The target has a Peace Shield, is already at war, has no Town Hall
    or no members online, or your colony is still on its cooldown. You
    need a War Banner in your inventory and to be Chairman or Commissar.

  "My citizens disappeared!"
    They only exist while the Town Hall area is loaded. Walk back to
    your colony and they return.

  "The State Chest is full."
    Make room, then /colony spoils to bring in the overflow.
